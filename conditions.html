Condition detail

Asthma

Asthma is a condition where your airways become narrow and swollen, making it hard to breathe. It is usually triggered by allergens, exercise, or cold air. While it cannot be fully cured, it can be well managed with proper medication. Most people with asthma live normal, active lives.

Pay close attention to warning signs like wheezing, chest tightness, shortness of breath, and coughing at night — early treatment can help reduce flare-ups and keep symptoms under control.

Common symptoms
Wheezing Chest tightness Shortness of breath Coughing at night
!

If you experience sudden difficulty breathing, wheezing, or chest tightness, please visit our hospital immediately — early treatment makes a big difference.

Asthma affects over 300 million people worldwide — you are definitely not alone in this!

COPD & Emphysema

COPD (Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease) is a long-term lung condition that blocks airflow and makes breathing difficult. Emphysema is a type of COPD where the tiny air sacs in your lungs get damaged. It is mostly caused by smoking. Treatment can slow the disease and greatly improve quality of life.

Common symptoms
Chronic cough Mucus production Breathlessness Fatigue Frequent chest infections
!

If you find yourself getting breathless with simple tasks like walking or climbing stairs, don't wait — come see us for a lung function test today.

COPD is the 3rd leading cause of death globally, yet over 70% of cases go undiagnosed until advanced stages.

Condition detail

Chronic Bronchitis

Chronic bronchitis means the airways in your lungs are constantly inflamed and produce too much mucus. This causes a long-lasting cough that usually lasts at least 3 months a year. Smoking is the most common cause. Quitting smoking and using inhalers can significantly reduce symptoms.

Common symptoms
Persistent wet cough Excess mucus Mild fever Chest discomfort
!

A cough that has lasted more than 3 weeks should never be ignored. Visit us — we can help identify the cause and start the right treatment.

Condition detail

Obstructive Sleep Apnea

Sleep apnea is a condition where your breathing repeatedly stops and starts while you are asleep. Your throat muscles relax too much and block your airway. It often causes loud snoring and leaves you feeling tired even after a full night's sleep. It is treatable with a CPAP machine or lifestyle changes.

Common symptoms
Loud snoring Gasping during sleep Morning headaches Daytime sleepiness Dry mouth
!

If your partner reports that you stop breathing during sleep, or if you feel exhausted every morning, please come in — untreated sleep apnea can affect your heart health.

Condition detail

Insomnia

Insomnia is when you have trouble falling asleep, staying asleep, or waking up too early. It can be caused by stress, anxiety, poor sleep habits, or other medical conditions. It affects your energy, mood, and overall health. Treatment includes therapy, lifestyle changes, and sometimes medication.

Common symptoms
Difficulty falling asleep Waking up often Feeling tired on waking Irritability Difficulty concentrating
!

If poor sleep is affecting your daily life for more than a few weeks, visit our clinic — we can help you find the root cause and a lasting solution.

Condition detail

Snoring

Snoring happens when air flows past relaxed tissues in your throat and causes them to vibrate. It is very common and can be harmless, but loud or frequent snoring may signal a deeper breathing problem during sleep. Lifestyle changes like weight loss and sleeping position adjustments often help.

Common symptoms
Noisy breathing during sleep Restless sleep Sore throat on waking Daytime fatigue
Condition detail

Chronic Cough

A chronic cough is one that lasts for 8 weeks or more in adults. It can be caused by many things including acid reflux, asthma, allergies, or infections. While it may seem minor, a persistent cough can be very disruptive and may signal something that needs treatment.

Common symptoms
Coughing lasting over 8 weeks Sore throat Runny nose Heartburn Hoarse voice
!

A cough that won't go away is your body asking for help. Please visit us so we can get to the bottom of it quickly.

Condition detail

Shortness of Breath

Shortness of breath (dyspnea) is the feeling that you cannot breathe in enough air. It can happen suddenly or develop gradually. It may be caused by lung disease, heart problems, anemia, or anxiety. It is always important to get it checked, as it is rarely something to ignore.

Common symptoms
Difficulty breathing Chest tightness Rapid breathing Dizziness Bluish lips or fingertips
!

Sudden or worsening shortness of breath is a medical emergency — please come to our hospital right away or call emergency services.

Condition detail

Pulmonary Fibrosis

Pulmonary fibrosis is a condition where the lung tissue becomes thick and scarred over time, making it harder to breathe. It is a progressive disease, meaning it worsens gradually. The exact cause is not always known. Treatment focuses on slowing the progression and managing symptoms.

Common symptoms
Dry persistent cough Breathlessness Fatigue Weight loss Clubbing of fingers
!

If you notice increasing breathlessness and a dry cough that won't go away, please visit us early — early diagnosis greatly improves outcomes.

Condition detail

Interstitial Lung Disease

Interstitial lung disease (ILD) is a broad term for a group of conditions that cause inflammation and scarring of the lung tissue. It makes oxygen transfer from air to blood less efficient. It can be caused by exposure to harmful substances, autoimmune diseases, or be of unknown origin.

Common symptoms
Progressive breathlessness Dry cough Fatigue Unexplained weight loss Aching muscles and joints
!

If you are experiencing worsening breathlessness or a persistent dry cough, please do not delay — come see us so we can run the right tests for you.

Condition detail

Lung Nodules

Lung nodules are small rounded growths on the lung that are found on imaging scans. Most lung nodules are harmless and caused by past infections. However, some may need monitoring or further testing. Finding one early is actually a good thing — it means any issue can be caught and treated promptly.

Common symptoms
Usually no symptoms Occasional cough Breathlessness (in larger nodules) Chest discomfort
!

If a scan has shown a lung nodule or your doctor has advised follow-up, please visit us — we will guide you through the next steps with care.

Condition detail

Lung Cancer

Lung cancer occurs when abnormal cells in the lung grow uncontrollably and form a tumor. It is one of the most common cancers but is most treatable when caught early. Smoking is the biggest risk factor, though non-smokers can also develop it. Modern treatments including surgery, radiation, and immunotherapy have greatly improved outcomes.

Common symptoms
Persistent cough Coughing up blood Chest pain Hoarseness Unexplained weight loss Repeated chest infections
!

If you notice any unexplained weight loss, blood in your cough, or a cough that keeps changing, please visit us immediately — early detection saves lives.

Condition detail

Pneumonia

Pneumonia is an infection that inflames the air sacs in one or both lungs, which may fill with fluid or pus. It can be caused by bacteria, viruses, or fungi. It ranges from mild to life-threatening. With prompt treatment, most healthy individuals recover well within a few weeks.

Common symptoms
Fever and chills Cough with phlegm Chest pain while breathing Shortness of breath Fatigue
!

High fever, difficulty breathing, and chest pain together are a sign to visit us right away — pneumonia responds well to early treatment.

Condition detail

Sarcoidosis

Sarcoidosis is a condition where small clumps of inflammatory cells (granulomas) form in the lungs and other organs. The exact cause is unknown but may involve an immune reaction to an infection or other trigger. Many people recover without treatment, but some need medication to manage symptoms.

Common symptoms
Shortness of breath Dry cough Fatigue Swollen lymph nodes Skin rashes
!

If you have persistent fatigue, unexplained swollen lymph nodes, or breathing difficulties, please come see us — we have the expertise to diagnose and manage sarcoidosis.

Condition detail

Pulmonary Hypertension

Pulmonary hypertension means the blood pressure in the arteries going to your lungs is abnormally high. This forces your heart to work harder and can lead to heart failure over time. It can be caused by other lung or heart conditions. While it has no cure, medications and lifestyle changes can help manage it effectively.

Common symptoms
Breathlessness on exertion Fatigue Dizziness Chest pressure Swollen ankles and legs
!

Swollen legs combined with breathlessness is a combination that needs urgent attention — please visit our hospital so we can evaluate your heart and lung function.

Condition detail

Bronchiectasis

Bronchiectasis is a long-term condition where the airways in the lungs become permanently widened and damaged, leading to a build-up of mucus. It often results from a past severe infection or other lung disease. It cannot be reversed but can be well managed with regular physiotherapy and medication.

Common symptoms
Daily cough with large amounts of mucus Frequent chest infections Breathlessness Chest pain Wheezing
!

If you are coughing up large amounts of mucus every day or have repeated chest infections, please come visit us — with the right care plan, your quality of life can improve greatly.

Condition detail

Respiratory Infections

Respiratory infections affect the airways and lungs and include conditions like the common cold, flu, and COVID-19. They can be caused by viruses, bacteria, or fungi. Most mild infections resolve on their own, but some — especially in older adults or those with existing conditions — require medical treatment.

Common symptoms
Fever Runny or blocked nose Sore throat Cough Muscle aches
!

If a respiratory infection is getting worse after a few days, or if you have a high fever and difficulty breathing, please come to our clinic — do not wait it out.

Condition detail

Occupational Lung Disease

Occupational lung diseases are caused by breathing in harmful substances like dust, chemicals, or fumes at the workplace over a long period. Examples include asbestosis, silicosis, and occupational asthma. These conditions can develop slowly over years. Early detection and removing exposure to the harmful substance are key to slowing progression.

Common symptoms
Progressive breathlessness Persistent cough Wheezing Chest tightness Fatigue
!

If your breathing problems seem to worsen at work or improve on weekends, that is an important clue — please come see us so we can investigate your work environment's impact on your lungs.
